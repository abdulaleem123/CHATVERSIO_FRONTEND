How do I book a call with the CEO?

Choose a date, time and meeting platform in the booking calendar.

Which businesses can use an agent?

Service businesses, online shops, agencies and software teams can start with a recurring customer enquiry.

Can you build around our workflow?

Yes. Discovery covers the questions, systems and handoffs specific to your business.

Which social channels can we connect?

WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger and Telegram can work alongside your website chat.

How long does setup take?

A simple setup can take days. Custom workflows may take one to two weeks, depending on integrations and testing.

Can we keep our phone number?

The team checks compatibility during discovery and agrees number ownership before setup.

What if the agent cannot answer?

You choose the fallback, such as collecting details for a callback. The team tests it before launch.

Can we review conversations in our CRM?

Yes. The Chat Agent can capture leads, show conversation history and let a person take over from the agent.

Can a person take over?

Yes. You define the handoff point and how the right person receives the conversation.

How are recordings and stored data handled?

Recording settings and retention periods are agreed during setup. The website privacy policy covers information submitted here.
