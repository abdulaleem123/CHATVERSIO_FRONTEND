The delivery process

Built with
your team.

Six steps from the first conversation to a working agent. Open a step to see what you’ll review.

01Discover

Trace a real enquiry.

Bring a call, chat or form submission. Together we identify where customers wait and what a good outcome looks like.

DeliverableAn agreed use case and success criteria.
02Design

Write the conversation.

Choose the questions, tone and fallback. Your team reviews the words that customers will actually hear or read.

DeliverableAn approved conversation flow.
03Build

Give it a working voice.

We build the agent and rehearse ordinary requests, ambiguous answers and situations that need a person.

DeliverableA working agent you can try.
04Connect

Put the details to work.

Connect the approved phone line, website, CRM or calendar. Test that each piece of information lands in the right place.

DeliverableA tested handoff into your tools.
05Launch

Open the line.

Review test conversations and agree who owns exceptions. Your team signs off on the first live workflow.

DeliverableA launch plan and a named owner.
06Optimize

Listen back and refine.

Review missed questions, handoffs and outcomes with your team. Improve the parts that matter to the customer.

DeliverableA prioritized list of improvements.
